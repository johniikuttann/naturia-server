This Cookie Policy explains how Naturia uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device. It lets the site remember things between pages and between visits — what is in your bag, whether you are logged in, which branch you selected.

We also use two related technologies:

  • sessionStorage — browser storage that is wiped the moment you close the tab. We use it to remember your selected branch for the current visit.
  • localStorage — similar, but persists until cleared. We use it to remember your cookie consent choice, so we do not ask you on every page.

Your consent

Strictly necessary cookies are set automatically, because the site cannot function without them. Everything else is off until you switch it on. Non-essential scripts do not run before you consent.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in the site footer. Rejecting non-essential cookies is as easy as accepting them — both options carry equal weight in our banner.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by services embedded in our pages rather than by us:

  • Razorpay and Stripe set cookies on their own payment pages to process your transaction and detect fraud.
  • Embedded maps, videos or fonts may set cookies when loaded.

We do not control these. Please refer to each provider's own cookie and privacy notices.

How to control cookies

  • On this site: use the Cookie Preferences link in the footer to change any non-essential category.
  • In your browser: all major browsers let you block or delete cookies. Look under Settings → Privacy. Blocking all cookies will break your bag and checkout.
  • To clear your branch: closing the tab clears the session value. Clearing site data removes the saved branch cookie.

Changes

If we add or remove cookies, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
