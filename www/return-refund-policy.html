We want you to be happy with what you buy. This page explains when you can return something, how refunds work, and how to cancel an order. Please read the non-returnable list before you order — electronics have some genuine exceptions.

The short version

  • You have {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} from delivery to raise a return request.
  • The product must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and the invoice.
  • Some categories cannot be returned once opened — see the list below.
  • A fault that appears later is usually a warranty claim, not a return. Both are explained below.
  • You can cancel free of charge any time before dispatch.

There is no statutory cooling-off period in India

Unlike some countries, Indian law does not give online shoppers an automatic right to change their mind and return goods. The window we offer below is a policy we choose to provide, not a legal minimum. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 if goods are defective, or not as described.

Return window

You may raise a return request within {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} of delivery. Requests after that cannot normally be accepted, though a manufacturer warranty claim may still be open to you for much longer.

Condition requirements

To be accepted, a returned product must be:

  • Unused and in resaleable condition
  • In its original manufacturer packaging, with the box undamaged
  • Complete with all accessories, cables, remotes, manuals, warranty cards and free gifts
  • Accompanied by the original invoice
  • Fitted with all original seals, tags and serial-number stickers intact

We may refuse a return, or reduce the refund, where the product is returned incomplete, damaged through use, or with a removed or defaced serial number.

Damaged or wrong on arrival

If a product arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered:

  • Check the outer packaging before you accept the delivery. If the box is visibly damaged, refuse it or note the damage with the delivery agent.
  • Tell us within {{DOA_REPORTING_WINDOW}} of delivery.
  • Send photographs of the product, the damage and the outer packaging.
  • Keep all packaging until the matter is resolved.

Where we are at fault, we arrange collection and cover return transport. You will not be charged.

How to raise a return

  1. Contact us on {{CUSTOMER_CARE_PHONE}} or {{CUSTOMER_CARE_EMAIL}} with your order number and reason.
  2. We confirm eligibility and give you a return reference.
  3. We arrange collection, or you bring the item to any of our six branches.
  4. We inspect the item on receipt.
  5. We approve and process your refund or replacement.

Please do not send anything back without a return reference — we may not be able to trace it.

Cancelling an order

Before dispatch

Cancel free of charge by contacting us as quickly as you can. A full refund is issued, including any delivery charge.

After dispatch

Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled directly. You can refuse the delivery, or accept it and raise a return. Where an order is refused at the door without a valid reason, {{REFUSED_DELIVERY_TERMS}}.

Cancellation by us

We may cancel where stock is unavailable, where there is a pricing or listing error, where we cannot deliver to your address, or where we suspect fraud. You will receive a full refund and we will tell you why.
