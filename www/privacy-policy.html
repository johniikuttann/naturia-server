Why we are allowed to use your data

Under the DPDP Act we rely on the following grounds:

  • Your consent — for marketing messages, analytics and non-essential cookies. You give this by an affirmative action, such as ticking an unticked box or accepting in our cookie banner. We do not pre-tick consent boxes.
  • Certain legitimate uses — where you voluntarily give us data for a purpose you clearly intend, such as giving your phone number so we can call you about a delivery.
  • Performing our contract with you — taking payment, delivering goods, honouring returns.
  • Legal obligation — GST invoicing, tax records, responding to lawful requests from authorities.

Transfers outside India

Stripe processes payment data on infrastructure located outside India. If you pay by card through Stripe, your payment data will be processed outside the country. Razorpay and Cash on Delivery do not involve such a transfer.

The Government of India may restrict transfers to particular countries under Section 16 of the DPDP Act. We will comply with any such restriction. If you would prefer your data not to leave India, choose Razorpay (UPI, net-banking) or Cash on Delivery at checkout.

Your rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction and completion of data that is inaccurate, misleading or incomplete.
  • Erasure of your data where we no longer need it for the purpose it was collected, unless we must keep it by law — tax invoices are one such case.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal is not retrospective; it does not undo processing already carried out.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer, whose details are below.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To exercise any of these, write to {{CUSTOMER_CARE_EMAIL}} or contact the Grievance Officer below. We may need to verify your identity before we act, so that we do not disclose your data to someone else.

How to withdraw consent

  • Cookies and analytics: reopen the cookie preferences link in the site footer and switch off any category.
  • Marketing messages: reply STOP to a WhatsApp or SMS message, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or email us.
  • Location: revoke the permission in your browser settings.

If we ever appoint a registered Consent Manager under the DPDP Act, you will be able to manage and withdraw consent through them, and we will update this policy with their details.

Children's data

Our website is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, as required by Section 9 of the DPDP Act. We do not carry out behavioural advertising or tracking directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

How we protect your data

  • The website is served over HTTPS.
  • Card and UPI credentials are entered on the payment gateway's own systems. We never see or store your full card number, CVV or PIN.
  • Access to customer records is restricted to staff who need it for their role.
  • Administrative access to the website requires individual accounts.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required under the DPDP Act.
